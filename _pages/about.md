Thermal Engineer · CFD · Electronics Cooling

I’m a thermal engineer focused on computational fluid dynamics, heat transfer, and electronics cooling. I’m interested in using simulation to understand how systems move heat, where thermal bottlenecks emerge, and how cooling approaches can improve performance.

My path to thermal engineering began with geosciences and atmospheric sciences. Studying complex natural systems strengthened my interest in fluid flow and heat transfer—questions I now approach through an engineering lens.
